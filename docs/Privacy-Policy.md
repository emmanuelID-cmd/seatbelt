# Seatbelt Privacy Policy

**Operating name:** Seatbelt by Kinoshi  
**Website:** https://useseatbelt.com  
**Privacy contact:** support@useseatbelt.com  
**Effective:** Upon account creation and acceptance

> **Legal review required:** This document is a working draft and is not legal advice. It must be reviewed by qualified counsel before publication.

## 1. Scope

This Privacy Policy explains how Seatbelt collects, uses, stores, shares, protects, and deletes information when you use the Seatbelt website, applications, software, and related services.

By creating an account or using the Platform, you acknowledge this Privacy Policy and the related Terms of Service.

## 2. Information We Collect

Depending on how you use the Platform, we may collect:

- name, email address, telephone number, domicile information, and account credentials;
- age and identity-verification results;
- limited identification metadata and document numbers;
- Driver license, vehicle, registration, insurance, and verification information;
- payment and transaction information;
- ride requests, pricing, cancellations, waiting periods, and trip details;
- GPS and location information;
- messages, notifications, support requests, reports, and account activity;
- camera and audio indicators, recordings, and related metadata where applicable;
- device, browser, IP address, security, diagnostic, and usage information;
- information provided by verification, payment, hosting, mapping, communication, analytics, or other service providers.

We aim to collect information that is reasonably necessary for the purposes described in this Policy.

## 3. How We Use Information

Seatbelt may use information to:

- create and manage accounts;
- verify age, identity, Driver eligibility, and vehicle information;
- connect Drivers and Riders and coordinate rides;
- provide maps, GPS features, trip updates, notifications, and communications;
- calculate, process, document, and support payments, refunds, credits, and disputes;
- operate, maintain, troubleshoot, and improve the Platform;
- detect fraud, abuse, security incidents, policy violations, and unauthorized access;
- investigate safety reports, property claims, payment disputes, and other incidents;
- operate camera, audio, and evidence-review processes where applicable;
- enforce the Terms of Service and feature-specific policies;
- comply with legal obligations and valid legal process;
- protect the rights, safety, property, and security of users, Seatbelt, and others;
- send service, security, legal, and account-related notices;
- send optional marketing or promotional communications where permitted.

## 4. GPS and Location Information

During an active ride or applicable waiting period, participating Drivers and Riders may view live location information to coordinate pickup, route progress, distance, and estimated arrival.

Seatbelt may collect and retain historical GPS information, including routes, arrival times, waiting periods, and related timestamps, for operations, safety, support, payment disputes, fraud prevention, investigations, and legal compliance.

Historical location information is restricted to authorized Seatbelt personnel and service providers with a legitimate business need. Seatbelt may disclose location information when required by applicable law or valid legal process, including a subpoena, court order, or warrant, as applicable. Emergency disclosure may occur only where legally permitted and necessary to address an imminent threat.

## 5. Camera and Audio Information

Camera presence and audio capability may be identified through icons displayed in Driver profiles before ride acceptance.

Personal or Driver-owned cameras may record video during passenger rides. Audio recording for those cameras is determined by the Driver and must be accurately indicated.

Seatbelt-owned cameras or cameras installed in Seatbelt-owned vehicles may operate whenever the vehicle is turned on or in Drive, including outside passenger rides. Seatbelt-owned cameras may record audio whenever operating, subject to technical limitations and applicable law.

Seatbelt may receive, store, review, and use recordings for safety, operations, verification, dispute resolution, fraud prevention, investigations, and legal compliance. Drivers may access footage from rides in which they participated. Riders may access footage from rides in which they were passengers. Users may not access recordings involving unrelated users.

Users must not edit, alter, falsify, doctor, tamper with, disable, or obstruct required recording systems or footage.

### Authenticated and Supplemental Cameras

When a Driver connects a camera to Seatbelt’s systems, Seatbelt may authenticate that camera for use during rides. The authenticated camera is the required primary camera for the applicable vehicle or ride.

A Driver may use additional side-cameras or other non-authenticated cameras as supplemental recording devices, subject to applicable law and Platform rules. Supplemental cameras may not replace, disable, obscure, bypass, or serve as a substitute for the authenticated Seatbelt-connected camera.

During a ride, a Driver must not rely on supplemental or non-authenticated footage to claim that the authenticated camera was unnecessary, unavailable, dismissed, or properly replaced. Disabling or avoiding the authenticated camera while using a vehicle displaying a camera indicator may violate Platform rules and may result in review, suspension, termination, or other lawful remedies.

Seatbelt may use authenticated-camera connection records, camera indicators, upload records, and related technical data to review compliance and ride-related disputes.

## 6. Identity and Eligibility Verification

Seatbelt may require users to verify that they are at least eighteen (18) years old and may require identity verification before allowing access to some or all Platform features.

Verification may include domicile information, government-issued identification, country-standard document or identification numbers, and a live visual or liveness check.

Seatbelt may use external verification providers. Seatbelt generally stores the verification result and limited metadata rather than the complete identification document. Identification images may be deleted after verification unless retention is reasonably necessary for safety, fraud prevention, disputes, investigations, legal claims, or legal compliance.

Any future biometric, skeletal, or artificial-intelligence liveness feature will require separate technical, privacy, and legal review before implementation. Where required by law, Seatbelt will provide additional notice and obtain consent.

## 7. Payment Information

Stripe processes payments for Seatbelt. Seatbelt remains responsible for Platform charges, ride fees, refunds, credits, and payment-related support.

Seatbelt does not intentionally store complete payment-card numbers when Stripe can process that information on its behalf. Payment information may be handled according to Stripe’s privacy practices and security procedures.

Seatbelt may retain transaction, receipt, refund, credit, and dispute records for accounting, payment support, fraud prevention, legal compliance, and other legitimate business purposes.

## 8. Communications and Notifications

Seatbelt may send service, security, account, legal, safety, payment, and agreement-update communications through the Platform, email, or text message.

Email and in-app update notifications may be enabled by default. Text-message updates require in-app opt-in. Users may change notification preferences in Settings.

In-app and web update notices may appear through the standard Bell notification icon. Notices may summarize changes, link to the full updated document, and link directly to the specific changed sections.

Turning off notifications may cause users to miss important information about payments, pricing, renewals, data practices, safety requirements, or legal changes. Seatbelt may still deliver notices where permitted or required by law.

## 9. How We Share Information

Seatbelt may share information with:

- Stripe and payment-related providers;
- identity, eligibility, and background-check providers;
- hosting, authentication, database, storage, mapping, communications, analytics, and security providers;
- authorized Seatbelt personnel who need access for legitimate business purposes;
- Drivers or Riders when necessary to coordinate an active ride;
- professional advisers, insurers, auditors, or successors in connection with legitimate business operations;
- law enforcement, courts, regulators, or other authorities when required by law or valid legal process;
- persons involved in an emergency where disclosure is legally permitted and reasonably necessary to address an imminent threat.

Seatbelt does not authorize users to access information about unrelated users. Service providers may process information only as necessary to provide services to Seatbelt and subject to appropriate contractual or legal safeguards.

## 10. Data Retention and Deletion

Seatbelt retains information only for as long as reasonably necessary for the purpose for which it was collected, plus any additional period reasonably justified by safety, fraud prevention, disputes, investigations, legal claims, accounting, payment processing, or legal obligations.

The target retention schedule is:

- account and profile data: while active, plus one year;
- payment and transaction records: up to seven years where reasonably necessary;
- support, dispute, and safety records: up to seven years after resolution where reasonably necessary;
- GPS and ride telemetry: approximately two years;
- camera and audio footage: approximately thirty to ninety days unless linked to an incident, dispute, investigation, or legal obligation;
- security logs: approximately two years;
- backups: up to one additional year.

These periods are targets, not guarantees. Actual retention may vary based on technical, operational, legal, and safety requirements.

Data may remain after account termination or deletion when reasonably necessary for payment, tax, accounting, safety, fraud prevention, disputes, investigations, legal claims, or legal compliance. This may include records relating to Riders, Drivers, and Seatbelt administrators or team members. Seatbelt will not retain personal information indefinitely without a valid purpose.

## 11. Account Controls and Privacy Requests

Users may freeze, deactivate, or delete an account through available account settings. Seatbelt may explain the difference between freezing or deactivation and deletion before the user confirms the selection.

Users may request an export of available personal information through an account-settings export function. Requests to correct information should be sent to support@useseatbelt.com and should identify the relevant date, time, information, requested correction, and reason.

Seatbelt may retain information after a deletion request when retention is reasonably necessary for the purposes described in this Policy or when required by law.

## 12. Security

Seatbelt uses reasonable administrative, technical, and organizational safeguards designed to protect information against unauthorized access, disclosure, alteration, loss, and misuse.

No storage system or transmission method is completely secure. Users must protect their passwords, use reasonable account security practices, and promptly report suspected unauthorized access or security incidents to support@useseatbelt.com.

## 13. User Rights and Choices

Depending on applicable law, users may have rights to:

- access or obtain a copy of certain personal information;
- request correction of inaccurate information;
- request deletion of personal information;
- request export or portability of available information;
- manage notification preferences;
- withdraw optional communications consent;
- object to or limit certain processing;
- appeal certain account or privacy decisions.

Some rights may be limited by legal, safety, fraud-prevention, payment, security, investigation, or record-retention requirements.

To submit a privacy request, contact support@useseatbelt.com or use the applicable account-settings controls. Seatbelt may verify the requester’s identity before completing a request.

## 14. Children

The Platform is intended only for users who are at least eighteen (18) years old. Seatbelt does not knowingly permit children under eighteen to create or use accounts. If Seatbelt learns that it has collected information from a person under eighteen in violation of applicable requirements, it may take steps to restrict the account and delete the information where legally appropriate.

## 15. Policy Updates

Seatbelt may update this Privacy Policy as the Platform, data practices, or applicable law changes.

Updates may be announced through the Platform, the website, email, or text message. In-app and web notices may appear through the standard Bell notification icon and may link to the full Policy and the specific changed sections.

Material changes may require renewed acceptance before continued access to affected features. If a user does not accept an updated Policy, Seatbelt may restrict affected account features. Silence or failure to respond is not affirmative consent where applicable law requires express consent.
