# Camera in-Vehicle Policy

/Camera-in-Vehicle Policy1. Driver DisclosureDrivers may indicate in their profile if the vehicle is equipped with cameras.Riders acknowledge that no further disclosure is required regarding the location, type, or operation of cameras within the vehicle.Riders assume responsibility for reviewing Driver profiles prior to ride acceptance.All accountability for awareness of camera presence falls on the Rider./2. Rider AcknowledgmentBy riding in a vehicle with cameras, the Rider assumes no expectation of privacy.Riders agree to the terms of camera presence by entering into the ride agreement.Riders may access Driver profile information, including camera status icons, for informed decision-making./3. Company-Provided (1st Party) CamerasThe Company may provide 1st party cameras for purchase and installation in Driver vehicles.Vehicles using Company-provided cameras must:Have the camera functioning at all times during ridesMaintain the camera in good condition; any damage, malfunction, or broken cameras must be immediately reported to the CompanyAllow Riders to view camera operation upon request if required for compliance or verificationCameras purchased through the Company will display an icon on the Driver Dashboard, indicating camera presence.The Company logo will be embedded in the bottom right of all Company-issued camera icons./4. Camera Footage UploadsAll footage from Driver-installed cameras (including Company-provided cameras) will be automatically uploaded to the Company server.The Company retains instant access to all footage for operational, safety, verification, or compliance purposes.Drivers must not tamper with, disable, or obstruct uploads.5. Prohibited ConductDrivers must not disable, tamper with, or remove Company-provided cameras during active ride service.Refusing to operate or report camera malfunctions while the camera is installed constitutes a material violation of platform rules.Misuse of cameras, including recording for unauthorized purposes, is strictly forbidden and subject to account suspension, termination, and potential legal action./6. Enforcement & LiabilityViolation of this policy may result in:Immediate suspension or termination of the Driver account (and associated Rider account if applicable)Civil or criminal legal action for misuse or privacy violationsForfeiture of any points, compensation, or benefits related to the ride or camera programThe Company assumes no liability for Rider or third-party claims arising from cameras installed in Driver vehicles. Responsibility for understanding camera presence is entirely with the Rider./7. Operational NotesDriver profile icons for camera presence should be visible to all Riders prior to ride acceptance.Company-provided cameras must remain active and functional during ride periods.Drivers are encouraged to notify the Company promptly if any hardware issues arise./Rider In-App Acknowledgment – Camera in VehicleTitle: Camera Awareness & ConsentText (display
